The charter

We, sailors, owners, sponsors, volunteers, partners and lovers of offshore racing, join our voices so that La Solitaire lives on: the race that, since 1970, has made French sailing the finest in the world. A race where the ranking rewards the sailor, never the budget.

We affirm that its fifty-seven editions form a common sporting heritage, built by thousands of sailors, fifty ports and half a century of public commitment. A capital that no player, whoever they may be, can capture or denature without consulting those who built it.

We ask for three things: a moratorium on the decision of 17 July 2026 and the opening of a genuine industry-wide consultation; a fair examination of the economic solutions that allow the race to live without denying its nature (one-design, open, formative); and, for the Ocean Fifty class, the creation of a great race under its own name.

Our approach is constructive: we are against no one. We are for a race, for a school, for an ecosystem. Every player, organiser included, has a place in the solution.

By joining the Collective, I support these principles. My name may be published, shared with institutions only, or remain strictly confidential: the choice below is mine.
