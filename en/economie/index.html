Economic analysis · The miscalculation

The economic value
of a monument

La Solitaire 2028: compared scenarios, precedents and the organiser's track record. A working document built on public data and explicit assumptions, to inform a decision that commits a fifty-seven-year-old asset.

Method. The mechanism described in this document is certain: it follows from the very structure of the race's value. The amounts are estimates. The 2028-2032 projections are built from the public structure of OC Sport Pen Duick's activity and explicit assumptions on event contribution; they are neither OC Sport internal accounts nor an independent valuation.

1 · SummaryThe equation

The central point. Beyond the shock inflicted on an entire community, moving to the Ocean Fifty will sever the brand from the sporting ecosystem that carries its legitimacy. The Class, the FFVoile, the training centres, the skippers, the project sponsors, the community, part of the partners and host territories: all of it will stay with the benchmark Figaro race. “La Solitaire du Figaro” will be nothing more than a name. Its monetisation capacity will erode from the very first edition, while a large share of the organisation costs remains fixed. This scenario is not economically viable: starved of what feeds it, the name will disappear, inevitably.

5 · The assetFifty-seven years: the rarity that makes the value

They shoot offshore races, don't they

Radically changing the product after fifty-seven years means breaking the very retention mechanism that allowed the asset to thrive for fifty-seven years.

The recent history of offshore racing shows it: creating a race is relatively common; keeping it alive for decades is rare. Races gathering great skippers, sponsors, territories and media coverage have nonetheless disappeared after a few editions. Longevity creates a specific value: recurring commercial relationships, prestige passed between generations, permanent renewal of competitors, and the ecosystem's capacity to reinvest every year in the same sporting benchmark.

The durable formula. The difficulty is not launching a race. The difficulty is creating a rendezvous that renews its participants, funders and public without losing what makes it identifiable. These races evolved: sponsors, boats, organisers or formats. But they kept a sporting core legible enough to pass their reputation from one generation to the next. That continuity is what turns an event into a recurring heritage asset. Into a monument.

What the survivors have in common

A clear sporting propositionA promise immediately understandable and comparable from one edition to the next.

7 · The precedentArkéa Ultim Challenge: creating without destroying

EditionFormatPublic indicatorsStatus
2024First edition, 6 skippers230,000 visitors; 1.25 M TV viewers at the start; 54 M€ of announced advertising equivalentSuccessful launch
2028Second edition confirmedArkéa, Brest, Brittany Region, Finistère and the Ultim Class mobilised againContinuation confirmed
The economic reading. The Arkéa Ultim Challenge was built as a new brand, with the Ultim Class, the owners and the territories. It benefits from a very strong pre-existing imaginary around giant multihulls and round-the-world records, notably the one popularised by the Jules Verne Trophy, without substituting itself for that trophy. The case demonstrates that a new event asset can be created alongside a historic asset when the sporting proposition is distinct: without cannibalising what exists, without destroying anything, without borrowing anyone's legitimacy. Nothing required taking someone else's name, and the launch succeeded.

9 · The outcomesThree coexistence scenarios

ScenarioDescriptionEconomic reading
AFigaro continuityLa Solitaire stays in Figaro with the current organisationLow risk; certain profitability; continuity of the historic asset
BCoexistence under two brandsLa Solitaire stays in Figaro with its community; the O50 race develops under its own brand, as an autonomous assetTwo distinct propositions; the name, the legitimacy and the community stay together; the O50 proposition is clarified; a logic comparable to the Arkéa Ultim Challenge / Jules Verne Trophy pair
CTwo competing racesOC Sport switches La Solitaire to O50; the benchmark Figaro race is created oppositeValue fragmentation; competition for partners, cities and media; this is the break-point scenario
The common economic criterion. The most efficient scenario is the one that maximises the combined value of existing assets while limiting the cost of rebuilding brand, community, partners and territories. Two scenarios out of three respect it. The July 2026 decision chose the third.

The name can survive the separation. Prestige, though, follows the system that keeps producing the sporting benchmark. In the long run, value concentrates where the pathway, the best competitors and the community remain.
